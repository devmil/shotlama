Privacy

A static site, and an app that keeps captures local.

This website uses no analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies or third-party scripts, and it loads no fonts or files from other servers. Its release list is a static file. The host, GitHub Pages, may process ordinary request data such as IP address, time, requested path and browser under its own policy.

The app

Captures, annotations, History and settings stay on your computer. ShotLama has no account and no cloud service, and it sends nothing to Devmil.

Saved and copied images carry no identifying metadata. Concealment is flattened into every output, so concealed pixels cannot be restored from the file.

The app connects to the network only to check for updates, and only when you start the check or turn on the weekly schedule. Its logs never contain capture pixels, recognised text, file paths, window titles or device names.
